Weekly rotation rules

When to rotate a product out
After 2 weeks as a hero, move it to supporting or retire it — even good products get audience fatigue. Always have the next hero product ready to step up.
When to promote a product up
If the wildcard gets strong click-through or saves in its first video, move it to supporting next week. If a supporting product's review video converts well, make it a hero.
Friday analytics check
Every Friday, check Shop click-through rate per product. The hero with the highest CTR gets the Friday deal drop and Spark Ad budget. The weakest supporting product gets swapped out Sunday.
Sunday product prep checklist
1Pick your 2 hero products — best sellers or newest drops with strong photos
2Pick 2 supporting products — items that pair well with the heroes or need exposure
3Pick 1 wildcard — something new you haven't posted before
4Upload clean product photos + lifestyle shots to Higgsfield for all 5
5Batch generate all 21 videos, tag each to the correct TikTok Shop listing before scheduling
